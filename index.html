Taehyeong Kim 김태형

Senior Engineer at Samsung SDS. Ph.D. in Industrial and Management Engineering, Korea University.

I am a Senior Engineer at Samsung SDS. I received my Ph.D. in Industrial and Management Engineering from Korea University in August 2025, where I worked in the Artificial Intelligence & Data Analytics Lab under the supervision of Prof. Sung Won Han. My dissertation, Multimodal Representation Learning and System Development Based on Large-Scale Vision-Language Models for Visual Reasoning, studied how to make vision-language models reason over images and video.

My research focuses on large vision-language models, multimodal large language models, and multimodal reasoning, including knowledge-based visual question answering, visual entailment, and multi-agent video understanding systems. I also work on time series analysis and anomaly detection, with applications in reliability prediction, failure forecasting, and industrial data.

Alongside research, I have led industry-academic projects with companies and public institutes, hold several Korean patents, and compete actively in applied AI challenges, holding the Champion tier on DACON with 15 competition awards.
